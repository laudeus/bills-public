Privacy Policy

Bills Dashboard · Effective November 2, 2026

Bills Dashboard is a personal bill and subscription tracker. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. If you have questions, contact us at hlaudeus@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, we receive your name and email address from the sign-in provider you choose (Google, or email and password via Clerk). We store your email address and display name to identify your account.
  • Bill information you enter. The bills and subscriptions you add — names, amounts, currencies, due dates, frequencies, payment sources, notes, and reminder preferences — are stored so the app can show them to you, calculate due dates, and surface reminders.
  • Payment records you enter. When you mark a bill as paid, we record the amount, date, and any note you add.
  • Usage data. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Basic server logs (such as request times) may be generated by our hosting provider in the normal course of operating the service.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to operate Bills Dashboard for you: to sign you in, to store and display your bills, to calculate due dates and reminders, and to keep your data separate from other users’ data. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

How your information is stored and shared

  • Your data is private to your account. Every bill, payment, and setting is tied to your user account. Other users cannot see your data.
  • Authentication (Clerk). Sign-in is handled by Clerk, Inc. When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with Clerk so your account can be created. Clerk’s handling of that information is governed by Clerk’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google’s handling of your information is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. We receive only your name and email address from Google, for account creation and sign-in.
  • Hosting (Cloudflare). The app runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores data in a Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare’s handling of data is governed by Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.
  • No other sharing. We do not share your information with any other third party, except as required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Your bills, payments, and settings are kept for as long as your account exists, so the app can track them over time. You can delete individual bills and payment records inside the app at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email hlaudeus@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we will delete it.

Security

Access to your account is protected by your sign-in provider (Google or Clerk email and password). Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Your bills are scoped to your account on every request, so one user cannot access another user’s data.

Children

Bills Dashboard is intended for adults managing household bills. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is handled, we will update the effective date above and, where practical, note the change in the app.
